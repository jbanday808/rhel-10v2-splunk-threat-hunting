HUNTING FRAMEWORK

MITRE ATT&CK

Connect adversary behavior to a testable hypothesis, required telemetry, investigation pivots, and detection coverage.

Open the workstream ↗

ATT&CK organizes the investigation. It does not collect events or run detections. Start with source health in Splunk, review a scoped candidate query, compare expected and observed results, and record a sanitized finding.

  1. Choose a behavior and hypothesis.
  2. Verify telemetry and source health.
  3. Review SPL and investigate alternatives.
  4. Record findings and coverage gaps.
