# Hunt / Incident Briefing — Six-Part Outline

- Case/hunt ID, owner, current time and audience:
- One-sentence mission question:
- Decision or assistance needed, from whom, and by when:

## 2. Scope and coverage

- Approved assets, services and UTC period:
- Sources actually reviewed and collection health:
- Important gaps, exclusions and confidence limits:

## 3. What the evidence shows

- Two or three material observations, each with evidence ID:
- What is inferred rather than observed:
- Relevant baseline and alternate benign explanation:

## 4. Impact and current action

- Mission/service/data impact and confidence:
- Incident/vulnerability disposition and accountable owner:
- Authorized actions completed and verified / actions pending approval:

## 5. Recommendation and tradeoff

- Proposed next step, scope and expected benefit:
- Operational/evidence risk, dependency and rollback:
- Owner and validation criteria:

- Open actions, owners and due/review times:
