# 08 — Official NIST References

**Framework version note (November 2026):** NIST AI RMF **1.0** remains the published baseline used for this documentation; NIST states a revision is in progress. Recheck the source at each substantive project update.

1. **NIST AI 100-1, AI Risk Management Framework 1.0** — four core functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
2. **NIST AI RMF Playbook** — voluntary suggestions, not an official required checklist. https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/
3. **NIST AI 600-1, Generative AI Profile** — additional generative-AI risk management considerations. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1
4. **NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2** — seven-step security and privacy RMF for information systems. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/37/r2/final
5. **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, Release 5.2.0 (August 2025)** — security/privacy control catalog, not automatically an approved baseline. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final
6. **NIST SP 800-53B** — control baseline guidance and tailoring. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/b/upd1/final
7. **FIPS 199** — security categorization of information and systems. https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/199/final

## Scope and attribution

All links are official NIST resources. These files are **project-specific interpretation and templates**, not official NIST-generated assessments, selected baselines, compliance certifications, or signed authorizations. Keep reference dates and publication versions visible during future maintenance.
